How treatment is chosen

The stage of the condition is established first — what is damaged, how far it has progressed and whether other structures in the knee are involved.

The structures involved determine which options are relevant, and your activity requirements determine what the treatment has to achieve.

The relevant options are then set out with their expected recovery, their risks and the alternative of doing nothing for now, and a decision is made with you with a review point agreed. [Consent process detail TBC]
