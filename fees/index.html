How surgical estimates are given

After the consultation, if a procedure is being considered, the clinic prepares a written estimate. It sets out the surgeon’s fee, the anaesthetist’s fee and the facility charge as separate items, along with implants and consumables where these apply.

An estimate is not a final bill. The final amount can differ if what is found during the procedure changes what is done, or if your length of stay changes. [Estimate validity period TBC]
