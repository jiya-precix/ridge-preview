How I decide

In Dr Lai’s own words.

When surgery helps

When the damaged structure will not recover on its own, and the knee is giving way, locking or stopping you doing something you need to do. I want to see that on examination as well as on imaging before I recommend an operation — a scan report on its own is not enough.

When I would wait

When symptoms are settling, when the findings do not explain the pain, or when the knee has not yet had a fair attempt at rehabilitation. Waiting is a decision with a review date attached, not the absence of one.

What I would do first

A full history and an examination of both knees. Imaging where it will change what we do, rather than as a matter of routine. Then the options set out together, including the option of doing nothing for now.
